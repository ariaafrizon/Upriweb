The Genesis of Uprijon in Web3

AN ARTIST-BUILT
UNIVERSE

Uprijon is more than an NFT project. It is a character-driven world connecting fine art, digital ownership, physical collectibles, exhibitions, experiences, games, and animation. Upriworld is the gateway into that universe, and every future chapter is designed to preserve the history and value of the Genesis collection.

Long-Term Ecosystem Roadmap

BUILDING A WORLD,
ONE CHAPTER AT A TIME

01

Protect the Genesis

BUILD THE FOUNDATION

Document the complete story from Uprijon's origin and fine art practice to Upriworld, its sold-out release, Uprimutant, exhibitions, and Uprimons. The official website becomes the archive for the artist, universe, collections, holders, and roadmap. Every release must expand the story, collector base, or ecosystem value.
02

Make Ownership Meaningful

REACTIVATE COLLECTOR CULTURE

Restore collector energy through early access, priority collecting windows, holder-only editions, exhibition privileges, and access to the artist archive—including sketches, rejected concepts, studio notes, behind-the-scenes material, and unreleased art.
03

Uprijon Is No Longer Alone

ENTER THE UPRIMONS

Introduce Uprimons gradually through silhouettes, sounds, fragments, tracks, eyes, and hidden shapes. Every character receives a name, species, personality, origin, relationship to Uprijon, visual traits, and a possible future physical form. The goal is lasting IP, not a rushed mint.
04

Collect the Art. Enter the World.

UPRIJON ART EDITIONS

Launch individual timed art editions outside the PFP collections. Each edition opens for a short window and then closes permanently. Digital editions may be paired with collector packs, physical cards, signatures, numbered variants, QR or NFC authentication, exhibition cards, and secret releases.
05

From Wallet to the Real World

PHYSICAL UPRIWORLD

Grow physical collecting in a deliberate order: trading cards, art prints, apparel, small collectibles, and designer toys. Toy releases can include a public edition, numbered artist edition, Genesis holder colorway, and one-of-one hand-painted pieces by Aria.
06

Physical and Digital Become One

ART, NFT & INTERNATIONAL EXHIBITIONS

Connect Uprijon's fine art identity with digital ownership through original paintings with NFT certificates, unique digital twins, NFT-inspired fine art, exhibition editions, and holder previews. The long-term goal is a global collector experience across galleries and Web3.
07

Your History in Upriworld Matters

UPRIJON COLLECTOR PASSPORT

Create a wallet-based passport that recognizes Upriworld, Uprimutant, Uprimons, Art Editions, verified physical cards, exhibitions, toys, and community participation. Non-financial Upri XP unlocks levels, access, achievements, and badges such as Genesis Collector, Mutated, World Explorer, Art Patron, IRL Explorer, and Day One.
08

Participate to Unlock

QUESTS & MINI GAMES

Turn participation into story-driven expeditions. A character can enter a location, return with an item, artifact, XP, lore, achievement, or Uprimons encounter. The future system should remain portable and independent of a single platform while legacy NFTs retain their place in Upriworld history.
09

Enter the Map

UPRIWORLD INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE

Build a simple interactive map and expand it gradually. Destinations such as Upri City, Mutant Zone, Uprimons Forest, Green Garden, Upri Gallery, and Toy Factory can unlock lore, art, hidden characters, quests, mini-games, collectibles, and drops.
10

The Universe Starts Moving

UPRIJON ANIMATION

Begin with short animated loops, grow into 30–60 second shorts, then two-to-five-minute episodes, and eventually a complete series. Holders may receive early screenings, credits, premieres, easter eggs, or cameos while the creative direction remains artist-owned.

Collector Recognition

HOLDER UTILITY

Utility grows with each collection while the Genesis remains the highest historical tier.

Tier 01

Upriworld Genesis

Highest access, Genesis-only editions, private previews, priority for toys and merchandise, anniversary claims, Passport recognition, cameo potential, and permanent historical status.

Tier 02

Uprimutant

Mutation exclusives, priority access to Uprimons, special editions, quests, and selected physical products.

Tier 03

Uprimons

Character experiences, game and lore access, expansion benefits, and future physical adaptations.

Tier 04

Art Edition Collector

An accessible entry point with Passport recognition, ownership history, possible card redemption, collecting privileges, and community access.
